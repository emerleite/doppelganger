how it works

Three steps. Nothing clever.

  1. 01

    Point it at a folder

    Pick any directory — your Downloads, your camera roll export, an external drive. Doppelganger walks the tree, hashes every image, and skips your Photos library bundle so nothing gets corrupted by mistake.

  2. 02

    It finds the twins

    First pass: byte-identical duplicates via a content hash. Second pass: visually similar — resized, recompressed, slightly cropped — found with perceptual hashing. The slider tells the app how aggressive to be.

  3. 03

    You choose what stays

    Every cluster ships with a suggested keeper (highest-resolution, oldest, original filename). Untick anything you want to keep. Hit Move to Trash. Files go to your system Trash — recover with Finder “Put Back” if you change your mind.

Built on czkawka's Rust core and Tauri 2, so the whole thing is a single small native binary. HEIC works out of the box — modern iPhone libraries are first-class. Your state is remembered across launches so you can scan, walk away, come back. The keeper heuristic is tested to a 100% mutation score because nobody wants their originals trashed.
